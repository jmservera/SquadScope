November 2026 opened with agent skills escaping the coding sandbox. [deepseek-ai/dsh-superagent-prototype](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/dsh-superagent-prototype) drew the week's largest new-project audience around greater autonomy, while [wangzan101/Mika](https://github.com/wangzan101/Mika) and [jackwener/DSH-Android](https://github.com/jackwener/DSH-Android) put agents directly on Android devices. At the same time, narrowly packaged skills began altering interfaces, reconstructing animations, generating diagrams, and turning machines into explorable simulations.

That accelerates last week's specialization of agent skills, but reverses its secondary emphasis. Hardware liberation and low-level portability no longer lead; application control, creative production, and software comprehension do. The throughline is not simply "more agents." It is the conversion of agent capability into portable, task-shaped components that can act on screens, codebases, and creative assets.

The unresolved problem carried over unchanged: assurance is trailing action. The press is asking for real-time monitoring just as repositories are granting agents broader computer and phone access. This week's projects make autonomy more tangible, but supply little visible evidence in the crawl of permissions engineering, behavioral evaluation, or audit infrastructure. The ecosystem is getting better at giving agents hands before proving it can reliably watch what those hands do.

## This Week's Trends

**Agents moved onto the device.** [wangzan101/Mika](https://github.com/wangzan101/Mika) operates Android apps and the web, while [jackwener/DSH-Android](https://github.com/jackwener/DSH-Android) packages screen reading, system settings, and automation into an APK. [modelflare-ai/AnyModelBot](https://github.com/modelflare-ai/AnyModelBot) broadens the pattern with local-first, multi-model computer use. For practitioners, mobile deployment turns abstract agent permissions into an immediate security and UX design problem.

**Skills became production artifacts.** [heydamir/unslop](https://github.com/heydamir/unslop), [patch-gg/pixelfx](https://github.com/patch-gg/pixelfx), and [NiazMorshed2007/reanimate](https://github.com/NiazMorshed2007/reanimate) package visual judgment, effects, and motion reconstruction as reusable agent capabilities. [caru-ini/stack-diagram](https://github.com/caru-ini/stack-diagram) does the same for architecture communication. This continues last week's professional fragmentation, but shifts from advisory expertise toward outputs that can be inspected and compared.

**Reverse engineering became an interface.** [haydenschmitty/floorplan](https://github.com/haydenschmitty/floorplan) maps codebases in plain English, [elvarofficial/teardown](https://github.com/elvarofficial/teardown) promises interactive 3D disassembly, and the established [morluto/rea](https://github.com/morluto/rea) anchors the category in the trending cohort. The practical value is reduced onboarding and analysis time; the risk is that persuasive visualizations can conceal incorrect inference.

**Creative reference data became productized.** [anhao/ai-pose-gallery-zh](https://github.com/anhao/ai-pose-gallery-zh), [paipiange/nvzhuang](https://github.com/paipiange/nvzhuang), and [HRuiCcc/RuiC-PoseAtlas](https://github.com/HRuiCcc/RuiC-PoseAtlas) cluster around poses, clothing, multilingual search, and image-generation workflows. Their geographic and linguistic concentration is meaningful, although overlapping asset counts suggest reuse rather than three wholly independent technical breakthroughs.

**Open systems work persisted outside AI.** [valentderah/totk-pc-recomp](https://github.com/valentderah/totk-pc-recomp), [Djihads80/Eglantine](https://github.com/Djihads80/Eglantine), and [Arjun-Ingole/hop](https://github.com/Arjun-Ingole/hop) keep last week's control theme alive through recompilation, emulation, and easier Linux installation. It is now a supporting current rather than the headline.

## Where Industry Meets Code

The strongest alignment is around agents becoming infrastructure. GitHub's Universe and Octoverse coverage describes agent-assisted development spreading across the stack; the crawl supplies concrete operator-facing pieces, from [deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness) and [affaan-m/ECC](https://github.com/affaan-m/ECC) to new visualization and creative skills. NVIDIA's local-AI framing also meets real developer activity in Android agents and multi-model assistants. These are category-level correlations, not evidence that the articles caused the projects.

The sharper convergence is also a contradiction. MIT Technology Review calls for monitoring agent actions in real time, while [wangzan101/Mika](https://github.com/wangzan101/Mika), [jackwener/DSH-Android](https://github.com/jackwener/DSH-Android), and [modelflare-ai/AnyModelBot](https://github.com/modelflare-ai/AnyModelBot) expand access to apps, settings, commands, and computers. Developer effort visibly favors capability; no comparable new-project cluster addresses audit trails or runtime policy enforcement.

The divergences matter. Press attention to simulation, robotics, scientific discovery, dementia research, and enterprise acquisitions has little direct support in this compacted repository sample. Conversely, the media narrative largely misses multilingual creative-reference collections and the continuing open-systems work in recompilation and emulation. Most automated press correlations are weak category matches, so they should not be mistaken for direct ecosystem response.

## Signal & Noise

The credible signal is clustered, bounded work. Android agents appear in independent implementations, skill packaging spans several concrete outputs, and reverse-engineering tools target code, animation, and machines rather than an undefined universal assistant. [OpenBMB/MiniCPM-OCR](https://github.com/OpenBMB/MiniCPM-OCR) is especially legible: it names a constrained task and includes correctness assessment in its scope. The top-topic counts reinforce a broader base in developer tools and automation, while AI and agent topics remain a substantial but smaller subset.

Attention metrics deserve skepticism. [deepseek-ai/dsh-superagent-prototype](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/dsh-superagent-prototype) reached 9,326 stars within roughly two days, an extraordinary launch that proves attention but not retention or technical maturity. [aschmelyun/print-my-profile-pic](https://github.com/aschmelyun/print-my-profile-pic) explicitly converts stars into a physical novelty and has only six forks, making it engagement theater rather than an engineering trend. The three pose-and-clothing galleries show genuine demand, but shared counts and overlapping assets weaken the claim of independent innovation. Trending repositories provide only lifetime stars because `stars_gained` is absent, so enormous totals for agent frameworks indicate ecosystem gravity, not weekly momentum. No clear star-farm or fork-inflation cluster matches the learned manipulation thresholds in the visible new cohort, but several launch curves still warrant follow-up.

## Blind Spots

Agent observability is the largest absence: there is no visible new cluster for action logs, permission boundaries, rollback, or behavioral testing, despite both press concern and increasingly consequential mobile access. Provenance is also thin. Creative skills and reference galleries can generate or transform assets, yet licensing, source lineage, and output attribution are not a visible technical theme.

The sample also underrepresents robotics and scientific tooling relative to industry coverage, and shows little new work on energy or cost measurement for autonomous workflows. Finally, local-first assistants are appearing, but interoperable identity and policy controls across models and devices are not. Those missing layers will determine whether portable skills become dependable infrastructure or merely convenient demos.

## The Week Ahead

Watch whether Android agents develop explicit permission models and reproducible task evaluations rather than accumulating integrations alone. The creative-skill wave should separate durable tools from prompt packaging as users demand measurable fidelity, provenance, and repeatability. Reverse-engineering interfaces are likely to keep expanding, but trust will depend on showing how conclusions were derived. November's next phase should be judged by whether oversight begins catching up with reach.
